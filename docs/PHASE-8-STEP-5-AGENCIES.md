# Phase 8 — Step 5: Agency Leads & Team Performance

## Implemented
- Separate **Agency → Agent performance & lead assignments** workspace, accessible only to active owner/manager accounts, with Dari, Pashto and English controls.
- Scoped 7-, 30-, and 90-day dashboards counting in-app incoming inquiries, open leads, unassigned leads, requests without an in-app response, initial replies within 24 hours, average first-response time, and in-app CRM viewing and won labels.
- Active team scorecards showing **currently assigned** inquiries and first-reply statistics within selected window; the measured reply may have been sent by any authorized advertiser representative, **not necessarily the currently assigned agent**. No claims that an external call or a property sale was completed.
- Lead queue showing last contact time and status. Owners/managers can open conversations and use existing assignment controls to assign or unassign leads to active members. Agency agents remain limited to their own assigned inquiries on the API.
- Manager-only performance API and scoped lead queue API, date parameter validation, capped data bounds of 1,000 inquiries and 10,000 messages, and honest reporting of truncation. No public user details, full message bodies, or unverified completed sales in the performance response.
- Automated unit and PostgreSQL integration tests for role isolation, limited agent scope and basic response-time semantics.

## Access and correctness
- Owners and managers see agency-level metrics and can assign leads.
- Agents can access only their assigned conversations; the agency performance dashboard endpoint denies them.
- Seeker, unverified account, inactive/revoked member and other agency have no access to agency metrics.
- CRM "won" is a team-managed tag only. Count and median/average first reply measures include only conversations and messages generated by the app; external WhatsApp, calls, leads and transactions are not counted.
- Period results and agent scorecards are bounded **samples** for large agencies, not comprehensive accounting reports. Dashboard flags sampled output.
